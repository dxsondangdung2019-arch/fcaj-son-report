During my internship, I had the valuable opportunity to attend two professional events. Each event was an enriching experience that broadened my technical horizons, provided practical industry insights, and helped refine my future career trajectory.

Below are the detailed descriptions of the events I attended:

---

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Knowledge Sharing Session

&emsp;**Date & Time:** June 13, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** In-person

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Description & Key Activities:**  
This was the first in-person event I attended, featuring four highly practical presentations bridging foundational technical concepts and career orientation:

1. _“From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner”_ – Presented by Mr. Danh Hoang Hieu Nghi (AI Engineer, AWS Community Builder).
2. _“What does a DevOps Engineer really do?”_ – An insightful deep dive into the daily responsibilities and real-world workflow of a DevOps Engineer.
3. _“A scalable URL shortening service on AWS”_ – A classic System Design case study presented by Mr. Dinh Trung Kien and Mr. Nguyen Minh Tho.
4. _“Real-world Stories and Culture in Multinational Corporations”_ – Practical workplace experiences and cultural insights shared by Mr. Dat Pham (Data Analytics Engineer) and Mr. Cuong Nguyen (Process Engineer).

&emsp;**Outcomes / Value Gained:**  
Through this event, I gained a comprehensive overview of career progression within the cloud computing ecosystem. The technical deep dives into AWS architectural design (scalable URL shortener) and the realities of the DevOps discipline significantly strengthened my technical mindset. Furthermore, the first-hand perspectives on professional corporate culture provided strong motivation and essential preparation for my career journey ahead.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS First Cloud AI Journey Community Day - June 2026

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00 – 12:00 | Saturday, June 27, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Online via YouTube (Original Venue: Bitexco Financial Tower, HCMC)

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Description & Key Activities:**  
The event offered fresh perspectives on how the AI wave is transforming enterprise operations. It featured technical sharing sessions and live practical demonstrations on:

- Cultivating a "Cloud Thinker" mindset in the modern tech landscape.
- Developing Voice AI solutions optimized for the Vietnamese language.
- Leveraging Dev Agents to automate DevOps pipelines and minimize Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR).
- Utilizing Amazon Q to streamline Human Resources (HR) operations.
- Securing Amazon Q deployments through an MCP server.

&emsp;**Outcomes / Value Gained:**  
I acquired practical insights into how enterprises deploy Generative AI (GenAI) into production workflows rather than keeping it purely conceptual. The key takeaway was understanding the crucial role of the "Human-in-the-loop" strategy in AI-assisted workflows, along with learning enterprise data security practices when integrating with Large Language Models (LLMs).
