### Week 1 Objectives:

- **Onboarding & Orientation:**
  - Connect and get acquainted with FCAJ program managers, mentors, and fellow interns.
  - Understand the rules, regulations, working ethics, and weekly report formats of the internship unit.
- **AWS Free Tier 2025 Program & Strategy:**
  - Understand the breakthrough changes in the Free Tier 2025 program (Free Plan vs Paid Plan, $100 initial + $100 bonus credits).
  - Learn strategies to complete "Money-Making" tasks and avoid cost "credit killers".
- **AWS IAM & Access Control Security:**
  - Master administrative structure using IAM Users and IAM Groups.
  - Implement advanced security with IAM Roles, cross-account/role switching, and Principle of Least Privilege.
- **Amazon VPC & Cloud Networking Fundamentals:**
  - Understand Amazon VPC architecture, subnets, Route Tables, Internet Gateways, and NAT Gateways.
  - Learn how to deploy Amazon EC2 instances within a custom VPC and secure them with VPC Firewalls.
